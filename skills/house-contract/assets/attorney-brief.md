# One-page brief for the buyer's attorney

Send after the first call. Fill the brackets. Mark anything uncertain "to be confirmed" instead of leaving it blank.

First call, three questions (not requests):
1. What does your standard buyer rider cover? Could you send it so we don't ask for things you already include?
2. Should we contact you or your paralegal, and by email or phone?
3. Which searches do you run before signing (title, municipal, violations), and can we see the results before we sign?
