# Visit checklist

Property: ______________________   Date: __________   Visit: first / second / final walkthrough

Tools: laser measure, 25 ft tape, 4 ft level, strong flashlight, outlet tester, phone camera.

## Must measure (write where exactly you measured)

- [ ] Basement clear height at the lowest beam or duct: ______
- [ ] Basement floor to outside ground level: ______
- [ ] Attic lowest / highest clear height: ______ / ______
- [ ] Main floor and upper floor ceiling heights: ______ / ______
- [ ] (If an addition is in question) setback to lot line: wall ______ eave ______ steps ______
- [ ] (If a wall might come out) wall thickness at the door frame: ______

## Every house

- [ ] Electrical panel: brand ______, main breaker amps ______, breakers or fuses
- [ ] Water pressure: two taps plus a toilet flush at the same time
- [ ] Heating type and age plate: ______; water heater age plate: ______
- [ ] Stand in the main room 30 seconds: listen, smell
- [ ] Open every closet: smell for damp
- [ ] Floors: noticeable slope, soft spots, loud squeaks (note where)
- [ ] Walls and ceilings: cracks, stains, fresh paint patches (note where)
- [ ] Windows: open and close two; fogged glass; rot at sills
- [ ] Outlets: test a few with the tester, especially kitchen and baths
- [ ] Roof and gutters from the street; downspouts discharge away from the house

## Older houses add

- [ ] Number of electric and gas meters (extra meters suggest a second unit)
- [ ] Basement: separate outside entrance, egress window, sump pit, water marks, a horizontal line near the floor where drywall was cut after a flood, odor
- [ ] Sewer cleanout location (ask if not found)
- [ ] Old oil tank signs: fill pipe or vent pipe on the outside wall or in the yard
- [ ] Driveway and curb cut width
- [ ] Street tree and sidewalk heave
- [ ] Safety: radiator covers, water heater relief valve discharge pipe, handrails on open stair sides
- [ ] Attic: old siding or shingles on today's interior walls (where the original house ended)

## Final walkthrough (within 24 to 48 hours of closing)

- [ ] Run the heat through a full cycle; look for leaks at pipes and radiators
- [ ] Run the dishwasher, washer and dryer
- [ ] Every faucet, toilet and shower
- [ ] Basement and attic again for new water marks
- [ ] Every agreed repair done, with licensed receipts and warranties in hand
- [ ] Included items present; seller's belongings gone
- [ ] Keys, remotes, manuals
- [ ] Wiring instructions confirmed by phone using a number you looked up yourself
